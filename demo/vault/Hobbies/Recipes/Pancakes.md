# Pancakes

Makes about 8 pancakes, 10 cm across.

## Ingredients

- 125 g all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 egg
- 240 ml milk
- 2 tbsp melted butter
- Pinch of salt

## Steps

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
2. In a second bowl, beat the egg, then add the milk and butter.
3. Pour the wet mix into the dry mix. Stir until just combined. Lumps are fine.
4. Rest the batter for 10 minutes.
5. Heat a pan over medium heat and add a little butter.
6. Pour about 60 ml of batter per pancake.
7. Flip when bubbles appear on the top and the edges look dry, about 2 minutes.

## Variations

- Blueberry: fold 80 g of fresh berries in after step 3.
- Banana: mash one banana into the wet mix.

## Why the rest step matters

The flour needs time to absorb the liquid. Skipping the rest gives thin, flat pancakes with less rise.
