# Game guide: Starfall Caverns

Starfall Caverns is a 2D exploration game with a lantern mechanic. Your lantern burns fuel, and dark areas damage you if the light runs out.

## Basics

- Lantern fuel capacity: 100 units. Each oil flask refills 40.
- Oil flasks cost 12 gold at the Ferryman's stall in the Upper Hollow.
- The save crystal in each zone restores full health and fuel.

## Zone order

1. Upper Hollow
2. Glimmer Shafts
3. Ash Reservoir
4. The Hollow Deep

Chapter 3 unlocks the Ash Reservoir after you return the Silver Compass to the Ferryman.

## Boss: Warden of Ash

- Health: 4,800
- Phase 1: throws embers in a wide arc. Dodge left, then roll through the gap.
- Phase 2 (below 50% health): the Warden summons two Ash Hounds. Kill the hounds first, they heal the Warden if they reach it.
- Phase 3 (below 15% health): the arena goes dark. Light the four braziers to reveal the Warden.

Recommended level: 14. Bring at least 5 oil flasks.

## Collectibles

- [x] Silver Compass (Upper Hollow, behind the broken cart)
- [x] Mapmaker's Ink (Glimmer Shafts, north ledge)
- [ ] Ember Lantern upgrade (Ash Reservoir, sealed door)
- [ ] All 12 star shards
  - [x] Shards 1 to 6, in the Upper Hollow and Glimmer Shafts
  - [ ] Shards 7 to 9, in the Ash Reservoir
  - [ ] Shards 10 to 12, in The Hollow Deep

## Tips

- Enemies in dark areas take double damage from your lantern.
- Do not waste fuel in lit rooms.
  - Turn the lantern off near save crystals.
  - Lit rooms cost nothing to explore.
- If you are stuck, check the ferryman's dialogue twice. He hints at the next key item.
